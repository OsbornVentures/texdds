# TexDDS Terms and Locality Research Notice

The complete Student Contract & Software Agreement is published at [https://www.texdds.com/terms/](https://www.texdds.com/terms/).

## Locality Research Limitations

- TexDDS has conducted substantial research and due diligence involving more than 200 Texas localities, municipalities, counties, courts, and related court routes.
- Research includes reviewing available official court and government pages, forms, directories, public records, and traffic-safety data; recording source links; and narrowing or excluding unsupported claims.
- Locality and court information may still be inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, unavailable, or inapplicable to a particular citation.
- Courts, agencies, forms, fees, deadlines, addresses, telephone numbers, eligibility rules, and online systems can change without notice.
- Due diligence describes a research process. It is not a warranty, certification, legal opinion, or guarantee of current accuracy.
- The exact court named on the citation controls. The student must confirm current requirements directly with that court before paying, requesting defensive driving, enrolling for ticket dismissal, or relying on a form or deadline.
- Taking a course does not itself create eligibility, secure court approval, or guarantee dismissal.
- Locality pages are general educational information, not legal advice. TexDDS is not a court, law firm, government agency, or representative of a listed court.

## Machine-Use Rule

Agents using `okf/localities/localities.json` or locality markdown must keep the limitations above attached to any procedural summary and must prefer the current official court named on the citation over generalized locality information.
