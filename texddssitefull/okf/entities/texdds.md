## Founder And Instructors

- Founder: David L. Hensley - Retired Texas Department of Public Safety highway officer who started Texas Defensive Driving School in San Angelo in 1986.
- Randy Hensley: Owner/Instructor; involved with defensive driver training since 1987. After a brief career in oilfield sales, Randy returned to San Angelo to help his father, David L. Hensley, teach defensive driving. His career has stayed in education, and his instructive skills were critical in the development of the online course.
- Gary Hensley: Owner/Instructor; continuously taught classroom defensive driving since 1992. The school’s August 2026 instructor biography reports that Gary had presented the defensive driving course more than 2,000 times. When Texas Defensive Driving School created its online course, Gary's classroom experience helped shape the online course curriculum.
