# Houston Defensive Driving

## Summary

Houston-area drivers should start with the exact court printed on the citation. If it names a Harris County Justice Court, use the exact precinct and place shown there. TxDOT's 2024 city table reports 66,236 total crashes in Houston, including 318 fatal crashes resulting in 339 fatalities and 1,304 suspected-serious-injury crashes resulting in 1,519 suspected serious injuries. Its separate DUI-alcohol city table reports 2,113 crashes, including 91 fatal crashes resulting in 104 fatalities and 80 suspected-serious-injury crashes resulting in 113 suspected serious injuries. These Houston city figures do not determine DSC eligibility or which court handles a citation. Use only the card for the court named on your citation.

- Region: Gulf Coast
- County: Harris County
- Nearby areas: Baytown, Conroe, Harris County Justice Courts, Pasadena, Pearland, Sugar Land

### Baytown

- Customer fragment: [https://www.texdds.com/texas/houston/#baytown](https://www.texdds.com/texas/houston/#baytown)
- Court: Baytown Municipal Court of Record
- Summary: If the citation names Baytown Municipal Court of Record, request DSC by the appearance date through the court's current in-person or online route. If the citation involves an accident or speeding 25 miles per hour or more over the limit, make the request in person. Confirm the current amount before paying.

1. Confirm that the citation names Baytown Municipal Court of Record and use the appearance date printed on it.
2. Review eligibility and use the dedicated city DSC request. An accident or speeding 25 miles per hour or more over the limit requires an in-person request.
3. Wait for the court's response and confirm the current amount and completion requirements before paying or taking the course.

### Conroe

- Customer fragment: [https://www.texdds.com/texas/houston/#conroe](https://www.texdds.com/texas/houston/#conroe)
- Court: Conroe Municipal Court
- Summary: If the citation names Conroe Municipal Court, request DSC in person, by mail or through the court's separate DSC signup by the appearance date. The court lists $144, or $169 for a school-zone citation, and gives approved drivers 90 days for the certificate and certified record.

1. Confirm that the citation names Conroe Municipal Court and act by its appearance date.
2. Use the DSC signup, mail or in-person route with the notarized affidavit, Texas license, insurance and current amount. Do not pay the fine or enter a payment plan.
3. After the court processes the request, submit the course certificate and certified driving record within 90 days.

### Harris County Justice Courts

- Customer fragment: [https://www.texdds.com/texas/houston/#harris-county-justice-courts](https://www.texdds.com/texas/houston/#harris-county-justice-courts)
- Court: Harris County Justice of the Peace Courts
- Summary: If the citation names a Harris County Justice of the Peace, use the exact precinct and place printed on it. Harris County lists sixteen separate courts and offers an eligible online DSC application. Do not pay the citation or take the course before the court approves the request.

1. Read the exact Harris County precinct and place printed on the citation. Do not choose a court from your city, neighborhood, ZIP code or the nearest courthouse.
2. Use the official directory to confirm only that named office, then select the exact filed case in Find My Case if the DSC option is available.
3. Apply for DSC and pay only the court costs requested for that option. Do not make an ordinary citation payment or take the course before approval.

### Pasadena

- Customer fragment: [https://www.texdds.com/texas/houston/#pasadena](https://www.texdds.com/texas/houston/#pasadena)
- Court: Pasadena Municipal Court
- Summary: If the citation names Pasadena Municipal Court, allow the court's published ten days after issuance before contacting it, then use the current-citation application in person or by certified mail by the court date. The form lists $144, or $169 for a school-zone citation, and a 90-day completion period after authorization.

1. Confirm that the citation names Pasadena Municipal Court, allow the published ten days after issuance and use the court date printed for the case.

### Pearland

- Summary: If the citation names Pearland Municipal Court of Record, request DSC in person, through a lawyer or by certified mail by the appearance date. The court does not accept online DSC payment, email or fax as the request. Call for the full current amount.

### Sugar Land

- Customer fragment: [https://www.texdds.com/texas/houston/#sugar-land](https://www.texdds.com/texas/houston/#sugar-land)
- Court: Sugar Land Municipal Court
- Summary: If the citation names Sugar Land Municipal Court, request DSC online, in person or by mail on or before the appearance date. The court lists $144, or $169 for a school-zone citation. Online customers must choose Explore Options, not Pay Now.

1. Confirm that the citation names Sugar Land Municipal Court and act on or before its appearance date.
2. Choose Explore Options online, or submit the signed form in person or by mail with the Texas license, insurance, plea and current amount. Never select Pay Now for a DSC request.
3. Within 90 days after approval, return the signed court-copy certificate and Type 3A record together through an accepted completion route.
