# Nacogdoches Defensive Driving

## Summary

Nacogdoches-area drivers should start with the exact court printed on the citation. TxDOT's 2024 Nacogdoches city row reports 592 total crashes, including 5 fatal crashes that resulted in 5 fatalities and 10 suspected-serious-injury crashes that resulted in 10 suspected serious injuries. Its separate DUI-alcohol city row reports 41 total crashes, including 1 fatal crash that resulted in 1 fatality and 2 suspected-serious-injury crashes that resulted in 2 suspected serious injuries. These are separate crash-event and resulting-person measures; they do not determine DSC eligibility or assign a court. Use only the card for the exact named municipal court or Nacogdoches County precinct printed on your citation.
