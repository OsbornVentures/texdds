# Tyler Defensive Driving

## Summary

Tyler-area drivers should start with the exact court printed on the citation. TxDOT's 2024 Tyler city row reports 3,396 total crashes, including 13 fatal crashes that resulted in 16 fatalities and 58 suspected-serious-injury crashes that resulted in 75 suspected serious injuries. Its separate DUI-alcohol row reports 79 total crashes, including 3 fatal crashes that resulted in 4 fatalities and 6 suspected-serious-injury crashes that resulted in 13 suspected serious injuries. These are separate crash-event and resulting-person measures; they do not determine DSC eligibility or assign a court. Use only the card for the exact named municipal court or Smith County precinct printed on your citation.

- Region: East Texas / Pineywoods
- County: Smith County
- Nearby areas: Bullard, Chandler, Jacksonville, Lindale, Smith County Justice Courts, Whitehouse
- Customer page: [https://www.texdds.com/texas/tyler/](https://www.texdds.com/texas/tyler/)

- Statewide availability: TexDDS serves eligible drivers in every Texas county, subject to court approval for ticket dismissal.

### Bullard

- Customer fragment: [https://www.texdds.com/texas/tyler/#bullard](https://www.texdds.com/texas/tyler/#bullard)
- Court: Bullard Municipal Court
- Summary: If the citation names Bullard Municipal Court, request DSC by the appearance date and obtain approval before taking the course. Confirm the current amount and school-zone terms directly with the court because the live page's wording is not reliable enough to restate.

1. Confirm that Bullard Municipal Court is printed on the citation.
2. Request in person or in writing by the appearance date with the listed plea, notarized request, Texas driver license and insurance.
3. Wait for approval, then return the completion certificate and DPS record within 90 days.

### Jacksonville

- Summary: If the citation names Jacksonville Municipal Court, request by the scheduled court date with the required plea, Texas driver license or permit, insurance and the amount set by the court. ### Smith County Justice Courts

- Customer fragment: [https://www.texdds.com/texas/tyler/#smith-county-justice-courts](https://www.texdds.com/texas/tyler/#smith-county-justice-courts)
- Court: Smith County Justice of the Peace Courts
- Summary: If the citation names a Smith County Justice Court, use the exact precinct printed on it. The county says to request DSC by the original due date, in person or by mail postmarked by that date, with the listed plea, documents and $146. After approval, return the signed certificate, official DPS record and affidavit within 90 days.

1. Match the exact Smith County precinct printed on the citation; do not select a precinct from your city, ZIP code, address or proximity.
2. Request by the original due date in person or by mail postmarked by that date, following the county traffic-page checklist.
3. After approval, return the signed certificate, official DPS driving record and affidavit within 90 days. Ordinary fine payment is separate.
