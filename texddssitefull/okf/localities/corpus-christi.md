# Corpus Christi Defensive Driving

## Summary

Corpus Christi-area drivers should start with the exact court and, for a Nueces County citation, the exact Justice precinct and place printed on the citation. TxDOT's 2024 city table reports 7,238 total reportable crashes in Corpus Christi, including 41 fatal crashes resulting in 43 fatalities and 111 suspected-serious-injury crashes resulting in 127 suspected serious injuries. Its separate DUI-alcohol city table reports 327 crashes, including 19 fatal crashes resulting in 21 fatalities and 19 suspected-serious-injury crashes resulting in 25 suspected serious injuries. These city traffic figures do not determine DSC eligibility. Use only the card for the court named on your citation.

- Region: Coastal Bend
- County: Nueces County
- Nearby areas: Ingleside, Kingsville, Nueces County Justice Courts, Portland, Robstown, Sinton

### Ingleside

- Customer fragment: [https://www.texdds.com/texas/corpus-christi/#ingleside](https://www.texdds.com/texas/corpus-christi/#ingleside)
- Court: Ingleside Municipal Court of Record
- Summary: If the citation names Ingleside Municipal Court of Record, request DSC on or before the appearance date and wait for permission before taking or paying for the course. The current materials list $144, or $169 for a school-zone violation, and a 90-day completion period.

1. Confirm that the citation names Ingleside Municipal Court of Record and request DSC by the appearance date.
2. Use the online request with the plea, license or military proof, insurance and required uploads; do not pay before the request is granted.
3. After approval, complete a state-approved course and return the certificate and driving record within 90 days.
