# Fort Worth Defensive Driving

## Summary

Fort Worth-area drivers should start with the exact court printed on the citation. If it names a Tarrant County Justice Court, use the exact precinct shown there. TxDOT's 2024 city table reports 12,865 total crashes in Fort Worth, including 106 fatal crashes resulting in 116 fatalities and 391 suspected-serious-injury crashes resulting in 460 suspected serious injuries. Its separate DUI-alcohol city table reports 414 crashes, including 13 fatal crashes resulting in 17 fatalities and 23 suspected-serious-injury crashes resulting in 34 suspected serious injuries. These city figures do not determine DSC eligibility or which court handles a citation. Use only the card for the court named on your citation.

- Region: DFW west core
- County: Tarrant County
- Nearby areas: Arlington, Benbrook, Burleson, Grand Prairie, Haltom City, Keller, Mansfield, North Richland Hills, Saginaw, Tarrant County Justice Courts, White Settlement
- Customer page: [https://www.texdds.com/texas/fort-worth/](https://www.texdds.com/texas/fort-worth/)

- Statewide availability: TexDDS serves eligible drivers in every Texas county, subject to court approval for ticket dismissal.

### Arlington

- Customer fragment: [https://www.texdds.com/texas/fort-worth/#arlington](https://www.texdds.com/texas/fort-worth/#arlington)
- Court: Arlington Municipal Court
- Summary: If the citation names Arlington Municipal Court, request DSC online, by mail or in person no later than the 30-day appearance date. The court lists $144, or $169 for a school-zone citation, and a 90-day completion package.

1. Confirm that the citation names Arlington Municipal Court and use the 30-day appearance date.
2. Submit the guilty or no-contest request, license, insurance in your name and current amount through a court-approved route. Full payment without the DSC option is a conviction.
3. Within 90 days, submit the TDLR course certificate, certified Type 3A record and signed affidavit.
