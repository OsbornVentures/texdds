# Dallas Defensive Driving

## Summary

Dallas-area drivers should start with the exact court printed on the citation and, for a Dallas County citation, the exact Justice precinct and place. TxDOT's 2024 city table reports 26,109 total reportable crashes in Dallas, including 207 fatal crashes resulting in 227 fatalities and 906 suspected-serious-injury crashes resulting in 1,081 suspected serious injuries. Its separate DUI-alcohol city table reports 809 crashes, including 61 fatal crashes resulting in 71 fatalities and 63 suspected-serious-injury crashes resulting in 102 suspected serious injuries. These city traffic figures do not determine DSC eligibility. Use only the card for the court named on your citation.

- Region: DFW east core
- County: Dallas County
- Nearby areas: Allen, Carrollton, Dallas County Justice Courts, Denton, Frisco, Garland, Irving, Lewisville, McKinney, Mesquite, Plano, Richardson
- Customer page: [https://www.texdds.com/texas/dallas/](https://www.texdds.com/texas/dallas/)
- Provider: [Texas Defensive Driving School](/okf/entities/texdds.md), TDLR license CP411
- Statewide availability: TexDDS serves eligible drivers in every Texas county, subject to court approval for ticket dismissal.

### Dallas County Justice Courts

- Customer fragment: [https://www.texdds.com/texas/dallas/#dallas-county-justice-courts](https://www.texdds.com/texas/dallas/#dallas-county-justice-courts)
- Court: Dallas County Justice of the Peace Courts
- Summary: If the citation names a Dallas County Justice of the Peace Court, use the exact precinct and place printed on it. The county lists ten separate courts. JP 1-1, JP 1-2, JP 3-1 and JP 4-1 have their own procedures below; the other six require a call to the exact court before payment.

1. Start with the exact precinct and place printed on the citation. Find only that office in the county directory; do not choose a court from your city, ZIP code or address.
2. JP 1-1: request by the appearance date and follow the 90-day order. Confirm the current amount; do not rely on the document's $146/$171, $0 or prefilled values.
3. JP 1-2: use the 2024 instructions and affidavit and the current Polk Street directory address, not the older address printed in the PDF. The instructions give 90 days but no safe current base fee, so confirm the amount.
4. JP 3-1: contact the current court first. Its linked request form is from 2021 and contains an old judge name and old amounts.
5. JP 4-1: request in person by the appearance date, wait for approval and follow the 90-day order. Its March 2026 sheet lists $146 or $171 subject to change, so confirm before payment.
6. JP 2-1, JP 2-2, JP 3-2, JP 4-2, JP 5-1 or JP 5-2: contact that exact office through the directory before paying and ask for its current DSC request, amount and deadline.
