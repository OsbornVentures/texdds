# McAllen Defensive Driving

## Summary

McAllen-area drivers should start with the exact court printed on the citation. TxDOT's 2024 McAllen city row reports 2,853 total crashes, including 6 fatal crashes that resulted in 6 fatalities and 55 suspected-serious-injury crashes that resulted in 65 suspected serious injuries. Its separate DUI-alcohol city row reports 130 total crashes, including 2 fatal crashes that resulted in 2 fatalities and 6 suspected-serious-injury crashes that resulted in 9 suspected serious injuries. These are separate crash-event and resulting-person measures; they do not determine DSC eligibility or assign a court. Use only the card for the exact named municipal court or Hidalgo County precinct and place printed on your citation.

### Pharr

- Customer fragment: [https://www.texdds.com/texas/mcallen/#pharr](https://www.texdds.com/texas/mcallen/#pharr)
- Court: Pharr Municipal Court
- Summary: If the citation names Pharr Municipal Court, request DSC orally or in writing on or before the appearance date and wait for court approval before completing the 90-day requirements.

1. Confirm that Pharr Municipal Court is printed on the citation and use its appearance date.
2. Request DSC orally or in writing with liability insurance, a valid Texas license or permit, a guilty-or-no-contest plea and the costs the court directs. The court's eligibility and offense exclusions apply.
3. Within the court's 90-day postponement, return the required driving record, affidavit and Texas-approved course completion certificate. The page does not publish an exact DSC amount.
