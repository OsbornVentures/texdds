# Longview Defensive Driving

## Summary

Longview-area drivers should start with the exact court printed on the citation. TxDOT's 2024 Longview city row reports 2,150 total crashes, including 7 fatal crashes that resulted in 7 fatalities and 37 suspected-serious-injury crashes that resulted in 39 suspected serious injuries. Its separate DUI-alcohol city row reports 67 total crashes, including 1 fatal crash that resulted in 1 fatality and 4 suspected-serious-injury crashes that resulted in 4 suspected serious injuries. These are separate crash-event and resulting-person measures; they do not determine DSC eligibility or assign a court. Use only the card for the exact named municipal court or Gregg County precinct on your citation.

- Region: East Texas
- County: Gregg County
- Nearby areas: Gilmer, Gladewater, Gregg County Justice Courts, Hallsville, Kilgore, Lakeport, Marshall, Tatum, White Oak

### Gilmer

- Customer fragment: [https://www.texdds.com/texas/longview/#gilmer](https://www.texdds.com/texas/longview/#gilmer)
- Court: Gilmer Municipal Court
- Summary: If the citation names Gilmer Municipal Court, use the current court-linked packet to request DSC in person or by mail by the appearance date. Wait for the court's approval before starting the course.

1. Confirm that Gilmer Municipal Court is printed on the citation and use the appearance date.
2. Submit the plea, request, license, insurance and $144 amount, or $169 for a school-zone offense, in person or by the packet's mail route.
3. After approval, return the signed completion certificate and DPS Type 3A record within 90 days.
