# El Paso Defensive Driving

## Summary

El Paso-area drivers should start with the exact court printed on the citation. If it names an El Paso County Justice Court, use the exact precinct and place shown there. TxDOT's 2024 city table reports 15,253 total reportable crashes in El Paso, including 66 fatal crashes resulting in 69 fatalities and 212 suspected-serious-injury crashes resulting in 243 suspected serious injuries. Its separate DUI-alcohol city table reports 472 crashes, including 21 fatal crashes resulting in 21 fatalities and 23 suspected-serious-injury crashes resulting in 30 suspected serious injuries. These city figures do not determine DSC eligibility or which court handles a citation. Use only the card for the court named on your citation.

- Region: Far West Texas
- County: El Paso County
- Nearby areas: Anthony, El Paso County Justice Courts, Horizon City, San Elizario, Socorro, Vinton
- Customer page: [https://www.texdds.com/texas/el-paso/](https://www.texdds.com/texas/el-paso/)

- Summary: If the citation names an El Paso County Justice of the Peace, use the exact precinct and place printed on it. The county lists eight separate Justice Courts; contact only the named office before paying or enrolling.

### Socorro

- Customer fragment: [https://www.texdds.com/texas/el-paso/#socorro](https://www.texdds.com/texas/el-paso/#socorro)
- Court: Socorro Municipal Court
- Summary: If the citation names Socorro Municipal Court, request DSC before the court date with the signed form, required plea, Texas license and insurance. For current citations, the page lists $144, or $169 for a school-zone offense, and requires the certificate and Type 3A record by the court-order due date.

1. Confirm that the citation names Socorro Municipal Court and use the court date printed on it.
2. Before that date, submit the signed DSC form, guilty or no-contest plea, valid Texas license, insurance and court-confirmed amount by mail, email or in person.
3. After approval, return the original completion certificate and original Type 3A driving record by the due date on the court order.
