# Wichita Falls Defensive Driving

## Summary

Wichita Falls-area drivers should start with the exact court printed on the citation. TxDOT's 2024 Wichita Falls city row reports 1,816 total crashes, including 7 fatal crashes that resulted in 7 fatalities and 14 suspected-serious-injury crashes that resulted in 20 suspected serious injuries. Its separate DUI-alcohol row reports 57 total crashes, including 3 fatal crashes that resulted in 3 fatalities and 1 suspected-serious-injury crash that resulted in 4 suspected serious injuries. These are separate crash-event and resulting-person measures; they do not determine DSC eligibility or assign a court. Use only the card for the exact named municipal court or Wichita County precinct and place printed on your citation.
