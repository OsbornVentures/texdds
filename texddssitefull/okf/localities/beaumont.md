# Beaumont Defensive Driving

## Summary

Beaumont-area drivers should start with the exact court, precinct and place printed on the citation and receive permission before enrolling. TxDOT's 2024 city table reports 3,926 total reportable crashes in Beaumont, including 20 fatal crashes resulting in 21 fatalities and 100 suspected-serious-injury crashes resulting in 116 suspected serious injuries. Its separate city table reports 59 DUI-alcohol crashes, including 2 fatal crashes and 3 suspected-serious-injury crashes. These city traffic figures do not determine DSC eligibility. Use only the card for the court named on your citation.

- Region: Golden Triangle
- County: Jefferson County
- Nearby areas: Groves, Hardin County Justice Courts, Jefferson County Justice Courts, Lumberton, Nederland, Orange, Orange County Justice Courts, Port Arthur, Port Neches, Sour Lake, Vidor

### Groves

- Customer fragment: [https://www.texdds.com/texas/beaumont/#groves](https://www.texdds.com/texas/beaumont/#groves)
- Court: Groves Municipal Court
- Summary: If the citation names Groves Municipal Court, request permission on or before the appearance date and before enrolling. A mailed request must be postmarked by that date and include the notarized affidavit, Texas license, insurance and the amount confirmed with the court. The course certificate and certified driving record are due within 90 days.

1. Confirm that the citation names Groves Municipal Court and request permission on or before the appearance date.
2. For a mailed request, use the notarized court affidavit with the required license, insurance and court-confirmed payment.
3. After approval, complete a state-approved course and return the certificate and certified DPS driving record within 90 days.
