# Midland-Odessa Defensive Driving

## Summary

Midland-Odessa drivers should start with the exact court printed on the citation. TxDOT's separate 2024 city rows report 6,288 total crashes across Midland and Odessa. Midland recorded 2,704 total crashes, including 19 fatal crashes that resulted in 20 fatalities and 60 suspected-serious-injury crashes that resulted in 68 suspected serious injuries. Odessa recorded 3,584 total crashes, including 28 fatal crashes that resulted in 29 fatalities and 65 suspected-serious-injury crashes that resulted in 76 suspected serious injuries. The separate DUI-alcohol rows report 389 total crashes: Midland recorded 182, including 1 fatal crash that resulted in 1 fatality and 11 suspected-serious-injury crashes that resulted in 15 suspected serious injuries; Odessa recorded 207, including 6 fatal crashes that resulted in 7 fatalities and 13 suspected-serious-injury crashes that resulted in 18 suspected serious injuries. These are separate crash-event and resulting-person measures; they do not determine DSC eligibility or assign a court. A Midland Municipal Court citation and an Odessa Municipal Court citation follow different processes, so use only the card for the exact named municipal court or county precinct printed on your citation.

### Andrews

- Customer fragment: [https://www.texdds.com/texas/midland-odessa/#andrews](https://www.texdds.com/texas/midland-odessa/#andrews)
- Court: Andrews Municipal Court
- Summary: If the citation names Andrews Municipal Court, respond by the tenth day and use the court's current forms page to reach its notarized DSC request.

1. Confirm that Andrews Municipal Court is printed on the citation and respond in person or by mail by the tenth day; a phone call is not an appearance.
2. Use the current court forms page to open the notarized DSC request. Include the listed plea, license and insurance materials, and contact the court for the current amount.
3. Wait for court approval. The linked form says to complete the course within 90 days and return the certificate and certified record by day 120, but the current court page controls the office address and current Texas-approved course terms.

### Odessa

- Customer fragment: [https://www.texdds.com/texas/midland-odessa/#odessa](https://www.texdds.com/texas/midland-odessa/#odessa)
- Court: Odessa Municipal Court
- Summary: If the citation names Odessa Municipal Court, request DSC by the appearance date with the listed plea, license, insurance and court-directed costs, then complete the approved case within 90 calendar days.

1. Confirm that Odessa Municipal Court is printed on the citation. Make the written guilty-or-no-contest request no later than the initial appearance or IA2 hearing.
2. Provide the listed license or qualifying military-family status, signed affidavit, current insurance and court-directed costs. The standing orders list violations that the clerk cannot approve at the window.
3. After court approval, return the signed, unaltered court-copy certificate and a DPS-certified driving record issued after the offense within 90 calendar days. Generic citation search or payment is not the request.
