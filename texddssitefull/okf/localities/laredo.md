# Laredo Defensive Driving

## Summary

Laredo-area drivers should start with the exact court printed on the citation. TxDOT's 2024 Laredo city row reports 6,512 total crashes, including 18 fatal crashes that resulted in 18 fatalities and 58 suspected-serious-injury crashes that resulted in 76 suspected serious injuries. Its separate DUI-alcohol city row reports 93 total crashes, including 3 fatal crashes that resulted in 3 fatalities and 1 suspected-serious-injury crash that resulted in 5 suspected serious injuries. These are separate crash-event and resulting-person measures; they do not determine DSC eligibility or assign a court. Use only the card for the exact named court, precinct and place on your citation.
