# Texarkana Defensive Driving

## Summary

Texarkana shares a name and a state line, but its Texas and Arkansas courts are separate. TxDOT's 2024 Texarkana, Texas city row reports 1,267 total crashes, including 2 fatal crashes that resulted in 2 fatalities and 14 suspected-serious-injury crashes that resulted in 15 suspected serious injuries. Its separate DUI-alcohol row reports 27 total crashes and zero fatal crashes, fatalities, suspected-serious-injury crashes or suspected serious injuries. These are separate crash-event and resulting-person measures; they do not determine DSC eligibility or assign a court. Use only the card for the exact Texas municipal court or Bowie County precinct and place printed on your citation. If the citation is from Arkansas, contact the Arkansas court printed on it instead.

- Region: Northeast Texas
- County: Bowie County

- Court: Bowie County Justice of the Peace Courts
- Summary: Use this card only when the citation names a Bowie County Justice Court and prints the precinct and place. The county lists five active offices: Precinct 1 Place 1, Precinct 1 Place 2, Precinct 2, Precinct 3 and Precinct 5. Precinct 4 is closed and is not a selectable court.

1. Match the exact Bowie County precinct and place printed on the citation. Do not choose an office from your city, ZIP code, residence or proximity.
2. For Precinct 1 Place 2, start with the live court page and its officially linked 2020 DSC request. Confirm the current amount and submission route before sending anything.
3. For Precinct 2, start with the live court page and its officially linked 2019 DSC request. Call the court before paying online or submitting the form.
4. For Precincts 1 Place 1, 3 and 5, call the exact office for current DSC instructions because no current court-backed workflow was found.
