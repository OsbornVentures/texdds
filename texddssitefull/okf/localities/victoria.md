# Victoria Defensive Driving

## Summary

Victoria-area drivers should start with the exact court printed on the citation. TxDOT's 2024 Victoria city row reports 691 total crashes, including 5 fatal crashes that resulted in 5 fatalities and 24 suspected-serious-injury crashes that resulted in 34 suspected serious injuries. Its separate DUI-alcohol row reports 55 total crashes, no fatal crashes or fatalities, and 5 suspected-serious-injury crashes that resulted in 10 suspected serious injuries. These are separate crash-event and resulting-person measures; they do not determine DSC eligibility or assign a court. Use only the card for the exact named municipal court or Victoria County precinct printed on your citation.

### Cuero

- Customer fragment: [https://www.texdds.com/texas/victoria/#cuero](https://www.texdds.com/texas/victoria/#cuero)
- Court: Cuero Municipal Court
- Summary: If the citation names Cuero Municipal Court, request DSC in person or in writing within the time printed on the citation. The current page lists $144 for a regular eligible offense and $169 for a school-zone offense, subject to confirmation by phone, and gives 90 days after approval to complete the course and return the certificate.

1. Confirm that Cuero Municipal Court is printed on the citation and use the date printed there.
2. Request in person or in writing with the listed license, insurance and court amount. Call the court to confirm the current amount before paying.
3. After approval, obtain the certified driving record and complete and return the course certificate within 90 days.

### Victoria County Justice Courts

- Customer fragment: [https://www.texdds.com/texas/victoria/#victoria-county-justice-courts](https://www.texdds.com/texas/victoria/#victoria-county-justice-courts)
- Court: Victoria County Justice of the Peace Courts
- Summary: Use this card only when the citation names a Victoria County Justice Court and prints the precinct. The county directory lists four separate offices and links a shared request form. The form must be received before the appearance date or sent by certified mail postmarked by the answer date, lists $146 and says not to take the course before the court issues an order. Confirm the current amount and instructions with the exact precinct before paying.

1. Match the exact Victoria County precinct printed on the citation. Do not choose one from your city, ZIP code, address or proximity.
2. Use the shared form linked by the current county directory and send it to that exact precinct before the appearance date, or follow the form's certified-mail postmark rule.
3. Confirm the current amount and instructions with the exact precinct, then wait for the court order before taking the course. Ordinary citation payment is separate.
