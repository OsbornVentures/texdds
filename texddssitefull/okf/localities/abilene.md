# Abilene Defensive Driving

## Summary

Abilene drivers should start with the court printed on the citation and ask for approval before enrolling. TxDOT reported 2,899 reportable crashes in Abilene in 2024, including 11 fatal crashes and 85 suspected serious injuries. Its separate city table reported 114 DUI-alcohol crashes. Use the matching court card below; nearby-city and county guidance applies only when that court is named on the citation.

- Region: Big Country
- County: Taylor County
- Nearby areas: Albany, Anson, Baird, Clyde, Hawley, Merkel, Sweetwater, Tuscola
- Customer page: [https://www.texdds.com/texas/abilene/](https://www.texdds.com/texas/abilene/)

### Baird

- Customer fragment: [https://www.texdds.com/texas/abilene/#baird](https://www.texdds.com/texas/abilene/#baird)
- Court: Callahan County Justice of the Peace Precinct 3
- Summary: If the citation names Callahan County Justice of the Peace Precinct 3, use the county's Baird office and its linked DSC affidavit. The linked instructions require the request package to be postmarked by the citation due date and give 90 days to complete the approved course and send the Court Copy of the certificate; confirm the current payment amount because the linked PDF is dated January 2020.

1. Confirm that the citation names Callahan County Justice of the Peace Precinct 3.
2. Use the county-linked affidavit and return the request, required copies, insurance proof and payment by the citation due date.
3. After approval, complete a Texas-approved course and send the Court Copy of the certificate within 90 days from the citation due date. The linked form does not require a driving record.
