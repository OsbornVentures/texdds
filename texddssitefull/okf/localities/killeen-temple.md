# Killeen-Temple Defensive Driving

## Summary

Killeen-Temple-area drivers should start with the exact court printed on the citation. Killeen Municipal Court and Temple Municipal Court use different local instructions. If the citation names a Bell County Justice Court, match both the exact precinct and place. TxDOT's separate 2024 city rows report 2,024 total crashes in Killeen and 1,765 in Temple, an arithmetic combined total of 3,789. Its separate DUI-alcohol rows report 92 in Killeen and 59 in Temple, an arithmetic combined total of 151. These figures do not determine DSC eligibility or assign a court. Use only the card for the court named on your citation.
