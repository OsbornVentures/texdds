# Austin Defensive Driving

## Summary

Austin-area drivers should start with the court printed on the citation and receive permission before enrolling. TxDOT reports 10,791 total reportable crashes in Austin in 2024, including 91 fatal crashes resulting in 98 fatalities and 349 suspected-serious-injury crashes resulting in 400 suspected serious injuries. Its separate city table reports 916 DUI-alcohol crashes, including 19 fatal crashes resulting in 21 fatalities and 43 suspected-serious-injury crashes resulting in 56 suspected serious injuries. These traffic figures do not determine DSC eligibility. Use only the card for the court named on your citation.

- Region: Central Texas
- County: Travis County
- Nearby areas: Bee Cave, Buda, Cedar Park, Georgetown, Hays County Justice Courts, Kyle, Lakeway, Leander, Manor, Pflugerville, Round Rock, Travis County Justice Courts, Williamson County JP2
- Customer page: [https://www.texdds.com/texas/austin/](https://www.texdds.com/texas/austin/)
- Provider: [Texas Defensive Driving School](/okf/entities/texdds.md), TDLR license CP411
- Statewide availability: TexDDS serves eligible drivers in every Texas county, subject to court approval for ticket dismissal.
