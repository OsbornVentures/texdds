# San Antonio Defensive Driving

## Summary

Start with the exact court printed on your citation. TxDOT's 2024 San Antonio city row reports 39,805 total crashes, including 162 fatal crashes that resulted in 170 fatalities and 596 suspected-serious-injury crashes that resulted in 686 suspected serious injuries. Its separate DUI-alcohol city row reports 1,394 total crashes, including 43 fatal crashes that resulted in 47 fatalities and 53 suspected-serious-injury crashes that resulted in 75 suspected serious injuries. Crash events and resulting people are separate measures; they do not decide DSC eligibility or assign a court. Use only the card for the exact municipal court or Bexar County precinct printed on your citation.

- Region: South Central Texas
- County: Bexar County
- Nearby areas: Alamo Heights, Bexar County Justice Courts, Boerne, Cibolo, Converse, Helotes, Leon Valley, Live Oak, New Braunfels, Schertz, Universal City

### Bexar County Justice Courts

- Customer fragment: [https://www.texdds.com/texas/san-antonio/#bexar-county-justice-courts](https://www.texdds.com/texas/san-antonio/#bexar-county-justice-courts)
- Court: Bexar County Justice of the Peace Courts
- Summary: Use this card only when the citation names a Bexar County Justice Court and prints Precinct 1, 2, 3 or 4. The county has four separate Place 1 offices; the exact precinct on your citation controls where every document goes.

1. Match the exact Bexar County precinct printed on the citation. Do not choose one from your home address, ZIP code, the stop location or proximity.
2. By the court date, request DSC from that exact office, complete the shared affidavit and provide insurance and the current amount that office directs.
3. Within 90 days of signing the affidavit, return the signed court-copy certificate and original driving record to the same precinct.
