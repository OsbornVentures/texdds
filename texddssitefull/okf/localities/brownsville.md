# Brownsville Defensive Driving

## Summary

Brownsville-area drivers should start with the exact court, precinct and place printed on the citation and receive permission before enrolling. TxDOT's 2024 city table reports 3,870 total reportable crashes in Brownsville, including 11 fatal crashes resulting in 11 fatalities and 75 suspected-serious-injury crashes resulting in 82 suspected serious injuries. Its separate DUI-alcohol city table reports 148 crashes, including no fatal crashes or fatalities and 12 suspected-serious-injury crashes resulting in 13 suspected serious injuries. These city traffic figures do not determine DSC eligibility. Use only the card for the court named on your citation.

- Region: Rio Grande Valley / Cameron
- County: Cameron County
- Nearby areas: Cameron County Justice Courts, Harlingen, La Feria, Los Fresnos, Port Isabel, Rancho Viejo, Rio Hondo, San Benito, South Padre Island
- Customer page: [https://www.texdds.com/texas/brownsville/](https://www.texdds.com/texas/brownsville/)
- Provider: [Texas Defensive Driving School](/okf/entities/texdds.md), TDLR license CP411
- Statewide availability: TexDDS serves eligible drivers in every Texas county, subject to court approval for ticket dismissal.

- Summary: If the citation names a Cameron County Justice Court, match the exact precinct and place printed on the ticket. Cameron County lists ten separate Justice Courts.
