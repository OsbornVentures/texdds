# Amarillo Defensive Driving

## Summary

Amarillo-area drivers should start with the court printed on the citation and ask for approval before enrolling. TxDOT reported 5,240 reportable crashes in Amarillo in 2024, including 17 fatal crashes resulting in 21 fatalities and 143 suspected-serious-injury crashes resulting in 172 suspected serious injuries. Its separate city table reported 235 DUI-alcohol crashes, including 3 fatal crashes and 26 suspected-serious-injury crashes. Use only the card for the court named on your citation.

- Region: Panhandle
- County: Potter and Randall Counties
- Nearby areas: Borger, Canyon, Carson County JP2, Claude, Hereford, Lake Tanglewood, Oldham County Justice Court, Pampa, Panhandle, Potter County Justice Courts, Randall County Justice Courts, Timbercreek Canyon, Vega
- Customer page: [https://www.texdds.com/texas/amarillo/](https://www.texdds.com/texas/amarillo/)
- Provider: [Texas Defensive Driving School](/okf/entities/texdds.md), TDLR license CP411
- Statewide availability: TexDDS serves eligible drivers in every Texas county, subject to court approval for ticket dismissal.

### Canyon

- Summary: If the citation names a Randall County Justice of the Peace, match the exact court number first.
