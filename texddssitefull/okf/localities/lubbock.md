# Lubbock Defensive Driving

## Summary

Lubbock-area drivers should start with the exact court printed on the citation. TxDOT's 2024 Lubbock city row reports 5,173 total crashes, including 26 fatal crashes that resulted in 29 fatalities and 60 suspected-serious-injury crashes that resulted in 71 suspected serious injuries. Its separate DUI-alcohol city row reports 247 total crashes, including 8 fatal crashes that resulted in 10 fatalities and 13 suspected-serious-injury crashes that resulted in 21 suspected serious injuries. These are separate crash-event and resulting-person measures; they do not determine DSC eligibility or assign a court. Use only the card for the exact named municipal court or Lubbock County precinct on your citation.
