# Galveston Defensive Driving

## Summary

Galveston-area drivers should start with the exact court printed on the citation. If it names a Galveston County Justice Court, use the exact precinct shown there. TxDOT's 2024 city table reports 1,483 total crashes in Galveston, including 8 fatal crashes resulting in 9 fatalities and 40 suspected-serious-injury crashes resulting in 50 suspected serious injuries. Its separate DUI-alcohol city table reports 76 crashes, including 1 fatal crash resulting in 2 fatalities and 6 suspected-serious-injury crashes resulting in 6 suspected serious injuries. These city figures do not determine DSC eligibility or which court handles a citation. Use only the card for the court named on your citation.

### Dickinson

- Customer fragment: [https://www.texdds.com/texas/galveston/#dickinson](https://www.texdds.com/texas/galveston/#dickinson)
- Court: Dickinson Municipal Court of Record
- Summary: If the citation names Dickinson Municipal Court of Record, request DSC in person or by mail by the appearance date. Call for the current amount, wait for written approval and submit the certificate and driving record within the court's 90-day period.

1. Confirm that the citation names Dickinson Municipal Court of Record and use its appearance date.
2. Submit the request, Texas license, insurance in your name and court-confirmed amount. A mailed request also needs the signed and notarized affidavit. Do not take the course before approval.
3. Return the certificate and driving record within 90 days from the affidavit date. Use the court email only for completion proof, not as an assumed initial request.

### Galveston County Justice Courts

- Customer fragment: [https://www.texdds.com/texas/galveston/#galveston-county-justice-courts](https://www.texdds.com/texas/galveston/#galveston-county-justice-courts)
- Court: Galveston County Justice of the Peace Courts
- Summary: If the citation names a Galveston County Justice of the Peace, use the exact precinct printed on it. The county lists four separate Justice Courts. Its Precinct 3 detail page currently shows another precinct's information, so use the elected-official directory and the corroborating official appraisal-district directory for Precinct 3.

1. Read the exact Justice precinct printed on the citation; do not choose a precinct from your city, address or the nearest courthouse.
2. Use the county directory to contact only that named precinct before paying or enrolling. For Precinct 3, confirm the current office through the elected-official directory because its dedicated detail page is mismatched.
3. Ask that exact court for its DSC eligibility, current amount, request deadline and completion terms. One precinct's process does not apply countywide.
