# San Angelo Defensive Driving

## Summary

San Angelo is home base for TexDDS, but your citation still belongs to the independent court printed on it. TxDOT's 2024 San Angelo city row reports 2,273 total crashes, including 7 fatal crashes that resulted in 7 fatalities and 33 suspected-serious-injury crashes that resulted in 40 suspected serious injuries. Its separate DUI-alcohol city row reports 76 total crashes, including 2 fatal crashes that resulted in 2 fatalities and 3 suspected-serious-injury crashes that resulted in 4 suspected serious injuries. These are separate crash-event and resulting-person measures; they do not determine DSC eligibility or assign a court. Use only the card for the exact municipal court or Tom Green County precinct printed on your citation.

- Region: Concho Valley
- County: Tom Green County
- Nearby areas: Ballinger, Bronte, Eldorado, Mertzon, Miles, Robert Lee, Tom Green County Justice Courts
- Customer page: [https://www.texdds.com/texas/san-angelo/](https://www.texdds.com/texas/san-angelo/)

- Statewide availability: TexDDS serves eligible drivers in every Texas county, subject to court approval for ticket dismissal.

### Tom Green County Justice Courts

- Customer fragment: [https://www.texdds.com/texas/san-angelo/#tom-green-county-justice-courts](https://www.texdds.com/texas/san-angelo/#tom-green-county-justice-courts)
- Court: Tom Green County Justice of the Peace Courts
- Summary: Use this card only when the citation names a Tom Green County Justice Court and prints the precinct. The county has four separate offices: Precincts 1 and 4 publish court-backed DSC forms, while Precincts 2 and 3 require direct court confirmation.

1. Match the exact Tom Green County precinct printed on the citation. Do not choose one from where you live, the stop location, a ZIP code or proximity.
2. For Precinct 1, start with the current forms page and its officially linked 2022 request form. Confirm every current term and amount with Precinct 1 before mailing anything.
3. For Precinct 4, use the current forms page and its 2025 application. Confirm the current terms, amount and payment method before sending anything.
4. For Precincts 2 and 3, call the exact office because no current court-backed DSC workflow was verified.
