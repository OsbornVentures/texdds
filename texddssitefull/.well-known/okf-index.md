## Product URL Policy

Course products resolve first to public customer URLs on `/courses/`:

- Texas Defensive Driving Course: https://www.texdds.com/courses/#defensive-driving
- Ticket Dismissal & Driver Record Bundle: https://www.texdds.com/courses/#bundle

- Texas Defensive Driving Insurance Course: https://www.texdds.com/courses/#insurance

Checkout endpoints are not canonical URLs and are intentionally gated behind the customer-facing Courses page.
