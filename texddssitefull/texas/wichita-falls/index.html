Red River / North Texas

Wichita Falls defensive driving and ticket dismissal.

Texas Defensive Driving School’s $25 online course (TDLR license CP411) covers the defensive driving step for Wichita Falls tickets. The courts below decide who qualifies and by when. Sheppard Air Force Base, Red River country, and the Hotter'N Hell cycling tradition give Wichita Falls a distinctive North Texas pulse. Wichita Falls Municipal Court requires the certified Type 3A driving record with the DSC request, not at the end of the completion period. In TxDOT’s 2024 city crash table, Wichita Falls recorded 1,816 crashes, including 7 fatal crashes and 14 suspected-serious-injury crashes. The court publishes $144, or $169 in a school zone, and the remaining area routes use their own contact and filing rules.

Wichita Falls Municipal Court of Record

Wichita Falls ticket-dismissal process

View Wichita Falls process Hide Wichita Falls process

If the citation names Wichita Falls Municipal Court of Record, request DSC by mail or in person on or before the appearance date. The current page lists $144 for a regular eligible offense and $169 for a school-zone offense, requires the plea, Texas noncommercial driver license, insurance, court costs and Type 3A record, and gives 90 days after approval for the completion certificate.

Court terminology

DSC, defensive driving, and similar labels

DSC
Driving Safety Course, the term most Texas courts use for the ticket-dismissal request.
Defensive driving, driver safety, or DDS
Common shorthand or page labels. Confirm that the clerk means the same DSC process before enrolling.
Deferred disposition
A different court option. It should not be treated as another name for DSC.

Wichita Falls Municipal Court of Record controls the option available for this citation. Ordinary fine payment is not a DSC request.

Court checklist

  1. Confirm that Wichita Falls Municipal Court of Record is printed on the citation and request DSC by mail or in person on or before the appearance date.
  2. Submit the guilty-or-no-contest request, Texas noncommercial driver license, current insurance, court costs and Type 3A driving record under the current court instructions.
  3. After approval, complete the course and submit the completion certificate within 90 days. Ordinary full payment is not the DSC request.

Wichita County Justice of the Peace Courts

Wichita County Justice Courts ticket-dismissal process

View Wichita County Justice Courts process Hide Wichita County Justice Courts process

If the citation names a Wichita County Justice Court, use the exact precinct and place printed on it. The county has five separate Justice Courts and does not publish one uniform countywide DSC process, so contact only the named office before paying or enrolling.

Court checklist

  1. Match the exact Wichita County precinct and place printed on the citation. Do not select a court from your city, ZIP code, address or proximity.
  2. Call only that named office for its current DSC eligibility, request documents, costs and deadlines before paying or taking a course.
  3. For Precinct 2, confirm the current mailing address because its page notes a temporary address during construction.
