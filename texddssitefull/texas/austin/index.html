Central Texas

Austin defensive driving and ticket dismissal.

Texas Defensive Driving School’s $25 online course (TDLR license CP411) covers the defensive driving step for Austin tickets. The courts below decide who qualifies and by when. The Capitol, live music, Longhorn Saturdays, and a fast-growing tech corridor all share Austin's crowded Central Texas roads. A Cedar Park address can still produce a Williamson County Precinct 2 case, so the address does not choose the court—the citation does. In TxDOT’s 2024 city crash table, Austin recorded 10,791 reportable crashes, including 91 fatal crashes and 349 suspected-serious-injury crashes. The fourteen reviewed routes span Travis, Williamson and Hays counties, with court-specific forms and deadlines.

Austin Municipal Court

Austin ticket-dismissal process

View Austin process Hide Austin process

For a citation filed in Austin Municipal Court, request DSC before the initial appearance date and wait for court approval. The current form requires the official request, insurance, a non-commercial Texas license or qualifying military proof, and $144 or $169 for a school-zone offense. After processing, return the certificate naming Austin Municipal Court and a DPS Type 3 or 3A record within 90 days.

Court terminology

DSC, defensive driving, and similar labels

DSC
Driving Safety Course, the term most Texas courts use for the ticket-dismissal request.
Defensive driving, driver safety, or DDS
Common shorthand or page labels. Confirm that the clerk means the same DSC process before enrolling.
Deferred disposition
A different court option. It should not be treated as another name for DSC.

Austin Municipal Court controls the option available for this citation. Ordinary fine payment is not a DSC request.

Court checklist

  1. Confirm that the citation names Austin Municipal Court and request DSC before the initial appearance date.
  2. Submit the current court form with the required insurance, license or military proof and payment; do not assume approval until the court processes it.
  3. Return the Court Copy certificate naming Austin Municipal Court and the required DPS driving record within the court's 90-day period.

Cedar Park Municipal Court

Cedar Park ticket-dismissal process

View Cedar Park process Hide Cedar Park process

For a Cedar Park Police citation filed in Cedar Park Municipal Court, request DSC with the plea, affidavit, license or qualifying military proof, named insurance, court costs and the $10 administration fee. Lakeway Municipal Court

Lakeway ticket-dismissal process

View Lakeway process Hide Lakeway process

If the citation names Lakeway Municipal Court, request DSC before the initial appearance date through the court-linked portal's Defensive Driving option. The court publishes $144 regular and $169 school-zone amounts, allows seven business days for paperwork, and requires a certificate naming Lakeway Municipal Court plus Type 3A record within 90 days. View Manor process Hide Manor process

If the citation names Manor Municipal Court, submit the current signed application, license, insurance and payment no later than the initial appearance date printed on the citation, and let the court process the request before any online payment. The current form and DSC page list $144 regular and $169 school-zone amounts, but the DSC page says 'second appearance date' while the form and Case Options page use the scheduled or initial appearance date.   3. Use the current $144 or $169 amount and confirm the exact deadline with the court because its live pages differ.

Williamson County Justice of the Peace Precinct 2

Williamson County JP2 ticket-dismissal process

View Williamson County JP2 process Hide Williamson County JP2 process

If the citation names Williamson County Justice of the Peace Precinct 2, use JP2's live criminal-forms page and notarized DSC request form. Do not pay the ticket online, because JP2 says online payment negates the opportunity for dismissal. The forms page and payment page differ on whether email or fax can carry a DSC request, so confirm the current submission route, total and completion deadline with JP2.

Court checklist

  1. Confirm the citation names Williamson County JP2 rather than Cedar Park Municipal Court or another court.
  2. Complete and notarize the current JP2 DSC form, then ask JP2 whether the request must be delivered by mail or in person or may use the forms page's email or fax routes.
  3. Do not pay online; contact JP2 for the current submission route, total and completion deadline.
