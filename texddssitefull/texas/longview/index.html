East Texas

Longview defensive driving and ticket dismissal.

Texas Defensive Driving School’s $25 online course (TDLR license CP411) covers the defensive driving step for Longview tickets. The courts below decide who qualifies and by when. Pineywoods roads, East Texas oil history, and Longview's ballooning tradition give this multi-county cluster a character all its own. The ten reviewed routes span four East Texas counties, and only three publish a current fee online. In TxDOT’s 2024 city crash table, Longview recorded 2,150 crashes, including 7 fatal crashes and 37 suspected-serious-injury crashes. Gilmer and Kilgore publish $144, or $169 in a school zone; the other current amounts come from the clerk.

Longview Municipal Court

Longview ticket-dismissal process

View Longview process Hide Longview process

If the citation names Longview Municipal Court, request DSC in person, online or by certified mail before or at the scheduled arraignment. Include the listed license and insurance documents and ask the court for the current amount.

Court terminology

DSC, defensive driving, and similar labels

DSC
Driving Safety Course, the term most Texas courts use for the ticket-dismissal request.
Defensive driving, driver safety, or DDS
Common shorthand or page labels. Confirm that the clerk means the same DSC process before enrolling.
Deferred disposition
A different court option. It should not be treated as another name for DSC.

Longview Municipal Court controls the option available for this citation. Ordinary fine payment is not a DSC request.

Court checklist

  1. Confirm that Longview Municipal Court is printed on the citation and use its scheduled arraignment.
  2. Submit the request, Texas license, insurance and the payment amount supplied by the court before or at arraignment. Ordinary ticket payment is not a substitute for the DSC request.
  3. After approval, complete the course and return the certificate and certified driving record within 90 days.
