Panhandle

Amarillo defensive driving and ticket dismissal.

Texas Defensive Driving School’s $25 online course (TDLR license CP411) covers the defensive driving step for Amarillo tickets. The courts below decide who qualifies and by when. Route 66, Panhandle cattle country, and the gateway to Palo Duro Canyon make Amarillo's wide-open setting unmistakable. Fourteen reviewed court routes cross the Panhandle, and many publish no current fee or deadline online. In TxDOT’s 2024 city crash table, Amarillo recorded 5,240 reportable crashes, including 17 fatal crashes and 143 suspected-serious-injury crashes. Randall County Precinct 1 publishes a complete process; Precincts 4 and 4A require direct confirmation instead.

City of Amarillo Municipal Court

Amarillo ticket-dismissal process

View Amarillo process Hide Amarillo process

If the citation names City of Amarillo Municipal Court, read the court's currently linked DSC Order No. 1 and confirm the current terms before acting. The linked order lists the affidavit, insurance and qualifying-license materials used for a request and the signed Court Copy certificate and DPS-certified driving record used for completion. Because the linked order is marked revised March 20, 2022, confirm the current fee and any payment-related deadline with the court.

Court terminology

DSC, defensive driving, and similar labels

DSC
Driving Safety Course, the term most Texas courts use for the ticket-dismissal request.
Defensive driving, driver safety, or DDS
Common shorthand or page labels. Confirm that the clerk means the same DSC process before enrolling.
Deferred disposition
A different court option. It should not be treated as another name for DSC.

City of Amarillo Municipal Court controls the option available for this citation. Ordinary fine payment is not a DSC request.

Court checklist

  1. Confirm that the citation names City of Amarillo Municipal Court and open the DSC order from the current standing-orders page.
  2. Before paying or enrolling, ask the court to confirm the current request method, amount and deadline.
  3. If the court grants DSC, return the signed Court Copy certificate and DPS-certified driving record by the deadline the court assigns.

City of Hereford Municipal Court

Hereford ticket-dismissal process

View Hereford process Hide Hereford process

If the citation names City of Hereford Municipal Court, enter the required plea within the city's 14-business-day period. The court lists a valid Texas license, certified driving record, insurance naming the driver and $144 for a DSC request, subject to its exclusions. Wait for court permission before taking the course.

Court checklist

  1. Confirm that the citation names City of Hereford Municipal Court and enter a plea within the 14-business-day period.
  2. Apply with the Texas license, certified driving record, insurance proof and $144 if the court's published exclusions do not apply.
  3. Wait for court permission before taking the course, then return the completion certificate as directed.

Randall County Justices of the Peace

Randall County Justice Courts ticket-dismissal process

View Randall County Justice Courts process Hide Randall County Justice Courts process

If the citation names a Randall County Justice of the Peace, match the exact court number first. JP1 alone publishes the listed DSC process: request by the answer date, submit its license, insurance, plea and affidavit items, pay its published $144 total, and return the certificate and certified driving record within 90 days. Contact JP4 or JP4A for their own requirements.

Court checklist

  1. Read the exact Randall County Justice Court number on the citation.
  2. For JP1 only, follow JP1's answer-date, $144 and 90-day requirements and use a state-approved course.
  3. For JP4 or JP4A, contact that office before the citation due date or before enrolling; do not reuse JP1's procedure.
