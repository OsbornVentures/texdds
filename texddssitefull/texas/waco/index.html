Waco defensive driving and ticket dismissal.

Texas Defensive Driving School’s $25 online course (TDLR license CP411) covers the defensive driving step for Waco tickets. The courts below decide who qualifies and by when. Baylor Bears, the Waco Mammoth site, Magnolia's Fixer Upper legacy, and SpaceX activity near McGregor all belong to the wider Waco-area story. Waco-area citations can route to eleven reviewed municipal and county court paths, each with its own request instructions. In TxDOT’s 2024 city crash table, Waco recorded 3,048 reportable crashes, including 80 suspected-serious-injury crashes. Most reviewed McLennan County courts publish $144, or $169 for a school-zone citation; McGregor and courts without a posted amount are exceptions.

Court terminology

DSC, defensive driving, and similar labels

DSC
Driving Safety Course, the term most Texas courts use for the ticket-dismissal request.
Defensive driving, driver safety, or DDS
Common shorthand or page labels. Confirm that the clerk means the same DSC process before enrolling.
Deferred disposition
A different court option. It should not be treated as another name for DSC.

City of Waco Municipal Court controls the option available for this citation. Ordinary fine payment is not a DSC request.

Waco Municipal Court’s published Driving Safety Course instructions.

Below are the City of Waco’s current Driving Safety Course instructions. Check the city page again before relying on a fee or deadline.

If you receive a citation for a moving traffic violation, you may be able to prevent it from appearing on your driving record by completing a Driving Safety Course. To qualify for this option:

  • You must possess a valid Texas driver's license and current liability insurance;
  • Can only be granted for one moving violation on the citation;
  • You must not have taken a Driving Safety Course in the past 12 months;
  • Could not have been speeding 25 miles or more over the designated speed limit; or
  • Could not have been speeding at 95 miles per hour or more; or
  • You must enter an appearance on the citation within 10 days from the issuance date of the citation.
  • You cannot possess a commerical driver's license.

You may request this option, by mail, online or by appearance in person, before the Municipal Court within 10 calendar days (not 10 working days) from the date you were issued the citation. You must present the following items to the Municipal Court:

  1. A plea of “No Contest” and the request to take a Driving Safety Course, either in writing (with your signature), which a space for your written request is provided for your selection on the back of the copy of the citation issued to you, or your appearance in person;
  2. A copy of your valid Texas Driver’s License;
  3. A copy of proof of current liability insurance - the proof of insurance must be in the name of the person that received the citation.
  4. You must submit a $144.00 fee or $169.00 if in a school zone, at the time you request to take the Driving Safety Course.

All of the above items must be presented at the time of the request to take a Driving Safety Course for this option to be granted by the court. You can mail all of the above to:

P. O. Box 2570
Waco, Texas 76702-2570

To ask questions, you may telephone the Municipal Court at (254) 750-5900

If mailing the above items, please be sure to include your name and address so the Order Granting the Driving Safety Course document can be returned to you with the compliance requirements and compliance due date.

After the court grants the Driving Safety Course, you must meet the two following compliance requirements.

  1. Take a Driving Safety Course within 90 days from the date the Order Granting the Driving Safety Course from a state certified instructor and return with your signature the certificate designated “Court Copy”. You will receive two certificates after completing the Driving Safety Course and the “Insurance Copy” is not to be returned to the court.
  2. Request a certified copy of your driving record from the Driver Records Bureau, Texas Department of Public Safety, P. O. Box 15999, Austin, Texas 78761-5999. This request must be mailed with a $10.00 fee, check or money order, made payable to the Texas Department of Public Safety. Please request the certified copy of your driving record be mailed to you and not to the court so you know the certified copy of your driving record has been received per your request.

The above two items must be returned to the court by your due date (the due date is on your Order Granting the Driving Safety Course document) for the violation to be dismissed and prevented from appearing on your driving record.

Robinson Municipal Court

Robinson ticket-dismissal process

View Robinson process Hide Robinson process

Robinson Municipal Court requires prior approval and both online forms before you take the course.

Submit the request and plea forms before the court date, then wait for approval and pay the court before enrolling.

Court checklist

  1. Complete the online Driving Safety Course Request and the separate Plea Options Form before the court date shown on the citation.
  2. Do not complete the course or submit completion documents until the court has approved the request and the fee has been paid.
  3. Provide insurance in your name, a valid Texas driver license, and $144—or $169 for a school-zone citation.
  4. Within 90 days, return the signed course certificate and a certified Type 3A Texas driving record.

Hewitt Municipal Court

Hewitt ticket-dismissal process

View Hewitt process Hide Hewitt process

Hewitt Municipal Court accepts DSC requests in person or by mail and uses the citation's appearance date as the filing deadline.

A mailed request must reach the court by that date, so allow enough delivery time for the complete packet.

Court checklist

  1. Include a signed guilty or no-contest plea and the court's Driver Safety Request form.
  2. Provide proof of liability insurance and a copy of a valid Texas driver license.
  3. Pay $144, or $169 for a school-zone violation, when requesting; the page specifies a money order payable to Hewitt Municipal Court for mailed requests.
  4. Return the completion certificate and certified Type 3A driving record within 90 days of the request.

McGregor's SpaceX test operations make rocket country literal; DSC requests still run through the Municipal Court on South Madison Street.

Request in person, by certified mail, or by email on or before the appearance date, following the court PDF’s channel-specific checklist.

Court checklist

  1. Provide a valid Texas driver license, insurance listing you as a driver, and the completed plea form.

  4. Contact note: the current court page and its linked DSC PDF publish different email domains. Use the current court phone or page before emailing.

Bellmead Municipal Court

Bellmead ticket-dismissal process

View Bellmead process Hide Bellmead process

Bellmead Municipal Court offers in-person, written, and online DSC requests tied to the appearance date on the citation.

Once approved, the case is deferred for 90 days while the driver completes the course and obtains a Type 3A driving record.

Court checklist

  1. Enter a no-contest plea, present a valid Texas driver license or the listed military exception, and provide insurance listing you as a covered driver.
  2. Pay $144, or $169 for a school-zone citation, and swear or affirm the court’s eligibility affidavit.
  3. The case is deferred for 90 days to complete the course and obtain a certified Type 3A driving record.
  4. The court page also offers a notarized plea-form mail route and an online application route.

Riesel began as a cotton-gin and railroad community on Highway 6; its current court still uses a form-and-mail DSC process.

China Spring Cougar country follows the court printed on the citation for DSC eligibility, deadlines, and filing instructions.

If the citation names a McLennan County Justice Court, use the county directory to find the correct precinct before requesting DSC.
