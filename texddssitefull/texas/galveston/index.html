Gulf Coast / Galveston Bay

Galveston defensive driving and ticket dismissal.

Texas Defensive Driving School’s $25 online course (TDLR license CP411) covers the defensive driving step for Galveston tickets. The courts below decide who qualifies and by when. Island life around the Seawall, the Strand, the port, and Galveston Bay puts tourism, shipping, and neighborhood traffic on the same roads. Galveston County's Precinct 3 route currently displays another precinct's information, so the elected-official directory is the safer identity check. In TxDOT’s 2024 city crash table, Galveston recorded 1,483 crashes, including 8 fatal crashes and 40 suspected-serious-injury crashes. The eleven reviewed routes around Galveston Bay publish different timing and payment rules—start with the court on the citation.

Galveston Municipal Court of Record

Galveston ticket-dismissal process

View Galveston process Hide Galveston process

If the citation names Galveston Municipal Court of Record, request DSC in person, by certified mail or through the eligible online option by the citation due date. For current citations, the court lists $144, or $169 in a school zone, and gives approved drivers 90 days to complete the requirements.

Court terminology

DSC, defensive driving, and similar labels

DSC
Driving Safety Course, the term most Texas courts use for the ticket-dismissal request.
Defensive driving, driver safety, or DDS
Common shorthand or page labels. Confirm that the clerk means the same DSC process before enrolling.
Deferred disposition
A different court option. It should not be treated as another name for DSC.

Galveston Municipal Court of Record controls the option available for this citation. Ordinary fine payment is not a DSC request.

Court checklist

  1. Confirm that the citation names Galveston Municipal Court of Record and use the due date printed on it.
  2. Submit the guilty or no-contest request, Texas license, insurance in your name and current amount through a court-approved route. In the portal, choose DSC only if that option appears; ordinary payment is not the request.
  3. After approval, complete the course and return the certificate and Type 3A driving record within 90 days.

Galveston County Justice of the Peace Courts

Galveston County Justice Courts ticket-dismissal process

View Galveston County Justice Courts process Hide Galveston County Justice Courts process

If the citation names a Galveston County Justice of the Peace, use the exact precinct printed on it. The county lists four separate Justice Courts. Its Precinct 3 detail page currently shows another precinct's information, so use the elected-official directory and the corroborating official appraisal-district directory for Precinct 3.

Court checklist

  1. Read the exact Justice precinct printed on the citation; do not choose a precinct from your city, address or the nearest courthouse.
  2. Use the county directory to contact only that named precinct before paying or enrolling. For Precinct 3, confirm the current office through the elected-official directory because its dedicated detail page is mismatched.
  3. Ask that exact court for its DSC eligibility, current amount, request deadline and completion terms. One precinct's process does not apply countywide.
