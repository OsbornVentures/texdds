Golden Triangle

Beaumont defensive driving and ticket dismissal.

Texas Defensive Driving School’s $25 online course (TDLR license CP411) covers the defensive driving step for Beaumont tickets. The courts below decide who qualifies and by when. Spindletop history, port traffic, refineries, and a strong Cajun influence shape the Golden Triangle around Beaumont. Across the Golden Triangle, ordinary online payment can close the dismissal path; Port Arthur expressly warns that payment waives DSC. In TxDOT’s 2024 city crash table, Beaumont recorded 3,926 reportable crashes, including 20 fatal crashes and 100 suspected-serious-injury crashes. Twelve reviewed routes span Jefferson, Hardin and Orange counties—ask the named court before paying.

Beaumont Municipal Court

Beaumont ticket-dismissal process

View Beaumont process Hide Beaumont process

If the citation names Beaumont Municipal Court, ask the court for permission before the appearance date and before enrolling. Submit the required plea, Texas license, insurance showing the defendant as covered and the amount the court currently assesses. The court gives 90 days for a state-approved course and the required DPS driving record. The court still links an application marked 09/2011, so confirm the current amount and requirements before using it.

Court terminology

DSC, defensive driving, and similar labels

DSC
Driving Safety Course, the term most Texas courts use for the ticket-dismissal request.
Defensive driving, driver safety, or DDS
Common shorthand or page labels. Confirm that the clerk means the same DSC process before enrolling.
Deferred disposition
A different court option. It should not be treated as another name for DSC.

Beaumont Municipal Court controls the option available for this citation. Ordinary fine payment is not a DSC request.

Court checklist

  1. Confirm that the citation names Beaumont Municipal Court and request DSC before the appearance date.
  2. Submit the required plea, valid Texas driver license, named liability-insurance proof and the amount the court currently confirms.
  3. After the court grants permission, complete a state-approved course and return the required certificate and DPS driving record within the court's 90-day period.

Hardin County Justice of the Peace Courts

Hardin County Justice Courts ticket-dismissal process

View Hardin County Justice Courts process Hide Hardin County Justice Courts process

If the citation names a Hardin County Justice Court, use the exact precinct printed on the ticket. The county directory lists six Justice Courts. JP2's form lists a $144 request package. JP5's form contains conflicting amounts, so ask JP5 for the current amount. These forms apply only to their named precincts.

Court checklist

  1. Read the precinct number on the citation and use that exact office in the county directory.
  2. For JP2 or JP5 only, use that precinct's linked form and request by the appearance date before taking a course.
  3. For JP5 or any precinct without a published form, contact the named court for the current amount, request route and due date.

Jefferson County Justice Courts ticket-dismissal process

Port Arthur Municipal Court

Port Arthur ticket-dismissal process

View Port Arthur process Hide Port Arthur process

If the citation names Port Arthur Municipal Court, request DSC on or before the appearance date in person, through counsel or by certified mail. Vidor Municipal Court

Vidor ticket-dismissal process

View Vidor process Hide Vidor process

If the citation names Vidor Municipal Court, request DSC by the response date or within 20 days of issuance, whichever is later; the court also publishes a 10-business-day grace period. The court lists $144 for a regular violation and $169 for a school-zone violation. For an electronic request, send the notarized form and documents first, then verify receipt and court approval before using the portal.

Court checklist

  1. Confirm that the citation names Vidor Municipal Court and review the court's eligibility list.
  2. Request in person, by mail or electronically with the court's form, license or military ID, insurance and correct payment.
  3. For an electronic request, call or email to verify receipt and approval before paying. Return the signed certificate and Type 3A record by the 90-day due date.
